## Completed stock movement workflow

### Backend

Protected APIs:

- `POST /api/parts/{part_id}/quantity-adjustments`
- `GET /api/parts/{part_id}/movements`

Supported operations:

- `add`
- `remove`
- `consume`
- signed `correction`

Behaviour:

- add records a `restock` movement
- consume records a `consume` movement
- remove and correction record `adjust` movements
- zero-value changes are rejected
- resulting total quantity cannot be negative
- resulting total quantity cannot be below reserved quantity
- `Part`, `StockMovement`, and `AuditLog` are written atomically
- movement records include before, after, delta, type, reason, note, source,
  actor, price/currency snapshots, and timestamps
- recent stock movement history is read-only
